Romania food importers & distributors

A working map of the companies most useful if Malee wants to put Thai juices, coconut water, canned fruit or plant milk on Romanian shelves — national FMCG distributors, beverage specialists, Asian/ethnic importers, and the two cash-and-carry chains that supply HoReCa and independent shops.

Best-fit partners for Malee

A shortlist for a first conversation, not an approved partner list. Reasoning is based only on public reach, category fit and the absence of an obvious juice-aisle conflict. No company on this page has been approached.

High fit

Aquila Part Prod Com

Listed national FMCG distributor in Ploiești, with organised trade, traditional trade, foodservice and vending. 2025 legal-entity turnover was 3.08 bn RON and the group preliminarily reported nearly 3.5 bn RON including recent acquisitions. It already carries food brands of its own and is not a competing juice manufacturer. Best general route into stores and HoReCa.

High fit

Interbrands Orbico

The largest FMCG distributor in the KeysFin retail/HoReCa ranking: 7.53 bn RON in 2025, about 2,300 employees, 200-plus brands and national coverage of hypermarkets, independents, petrol and HoReCa. Reach is excellent; Malee would be one principal among many, including tobacco and beauty. Worth a conversation for scale.

High fit · specialist

Belfoods Impex

A much smaller importer (16.4 m RON, 11 staff) that already brings in Asian groceries, including from Thailand, and says it is listed in Carrefour, Kaufland, Auchan and Mega Image. The closest public category match for coconut water, canned fruit and Asian grocery, if Malee wants a specialist rather than a giant generalist.

Good specialist

Everbio Distribution

Organic and health-food importer in Arad county (9.3 m RON). Its public catalogue already includes Thai Coco coconut drinks, so the coconut-water listing path is proven, even if the company is small and regional. A complementary partner, not a national exclusive.

Good for HoReCa

Marathon Distribution Group

A 1.21 bn RON food and foodservice distributor in Bucharest, with a large SKU book for retail and HoReCa. Useful if the first Malee volumes are aimed at hotels, restaurants and cash-and-carry rather than a full supermarket rollout. The pin is the registered office, which may not be the warehouse.

Listing targets

METRO and Selgros

The two cash-and-carry chains (11.91 bn and 4.46 bn RON) are not exclusive distributors, but they are the wholesale shelves used by HoReCa and independent grocers. A listing here sits alongside a distributor relationship, not instead of one. USDA also notes that the large grocery multiples import directly.

Coca-Cola HBC, Maspex (Tymbark) and Quadrant-Amroq (PepsiCo) are mapped because they dominate the juice and soft-drink aisle, but they are manufacturer-bottlers and are treated as category competitors, not candidate partners. Cyrom is the exclusive Pfanner importer — a juice specialist with the same conflict. Macromex (frozen/foodservice) and DRIM Daniel (traditional-trade FMCG, Żabka group) are credible second-wave options. Maresi is a branded frozen-food broker; Yamato Express is a small Japanese/Asian HoReCa importer.

Methodology and caveats

  • What this is. A public-source shortlist for discussion inside Malee, compiled on 17 November 2026. It is not a due-diligence file, not a credit opinion, and not a claim that any firm wants to represent Malee.
  • How firms were chosen. National FMCG distributors named in the 2025 KeysFin / Modern Buyer and Ziarul Financiar rankings (Interbrands Orbico, Aquila, DRIM Daniel, plus Marathon and Macromex); the two cash-and-carry wholesalers (METRO, Selgros); the three large beverage bottlers by 2025 CAEN 1107 turnover (Coca-Cola HBC, Quadrant-Amroq, Maspex) and the Pfanner importer Cyrom; specialist importers with a published Asian, Thai or coconut-water offer (Belfoods, Everbio, Yamato); and Maresi Foodbroker as a mid-size international brand broker.
  • What was left out, on purpose. Tobacco-heavy names that appear in some FMCG distributor rankings — notably Luzan Logistic (1.52 bn RON, 2025) and Punctual Comimpex (1.39 bn RON, 2025) — because their core business is tobacco, not food. Grocery multiples (Lidl, Kaufland, Profi, Carrefour, Mega Image, Penny, Auchan) often import directly, as the USDA FAS Romania Exporter Guide 2026 states; their retail turnover would dominate the map, so they are not plotted. Rauch Romania SRL was struck off in 2024 and is omitted. A trading site, Asian Share, offers Foco juices and Thai ingredients, but a matching legal entity was not confirmed in the registry extracts used, so it is not in the table.
  • Figures. Turnover is cifra de afaceri for calendar 2025 from annual accounts, as republished by Verti / Risco from Ministry of Finance and ONRC data (registry extracts checked in mid-November 2026). Employee counts are the average number of employees reported for 2025. For Aquila, the legal-entity figure (3,080,897,206 RON) is shown; the group’s preliminary 2025 revenue, including acquired companies, was “nearly RON 3.5 billion” in a Bucharest Stock Exchange report. Euro amounts are converted at the BNR 2025 annual average of 5.0415 RON/EUR (as published on cursbnr.ro) and labelled as conversions.
  • Map. Coordinates come from the OpenStreetMap Nominatim geocoder, queried on 17 November 2026. Pins marked approximate are at street, village or town level when the exact building was not found. Marathon’s pin is its registered office (an address that may be administrative rather than a warehouse). Belfoods is geocoded to building number 256 on Bulevardul Basarabia; OpenStreetMap labels a school at that number, so the pin may be the plot rather than a dedicated office.
  • Gaps. A large, independently verified fresh-produce importer was not confirmed to the same standard, so the “fresh” category is empty. Brand lists are only those named on a company site or in a cited article — they are not complete catalogues. Contact people and buyer emails are not published here. DRIM Daniel reported a net loss and negative equity in 2025; Interbrands Orbico and Selgros also reported a 2025 net loss. Those facts are on the filings and should be checked before any commercial commitment.
